  1. Tap words in the passage to keep them. The poem follows the original reading order, including the punctuation attached to each word.
  2. Under Give it room to breathe, tap a chosen word to place a line break after it. All controls also work with Tab and the usual button keys.

  4. Export page saves the finished SVG. Save keeps the complete editable notebook; Open restores that notebook later.
