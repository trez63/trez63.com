Automata Sonata

Paint cells, watch them evolve, and hear the grid play. Each column drives an oscillator whose volume follows local density.

  • Click / tap / drag – toggle or paint cells
  • Space – play / pause
  • S – single step
  • C – clear
  • R – randomize
  • H or ? – this help
  • T – theme

Edit Birth/Survive rules (comma-separated numbers). Save/Open stores the entire state as JSON. Audio unlocks on first interaction.
