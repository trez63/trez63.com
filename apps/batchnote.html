Offline · Flour is the reference

From ratios to a real batch.

  1. Enter the total batch weight, including flour and every addition. Choose 1–40 portions.
  2. Flour is the reference at 100%. Water at 65%, for example, means 65 g of water for every 100 g of flour. Enter your own formula’s ratios.
  3. Weigh the amounts in the list and check them off. A changed target or ingredient ratio clears the checks so they refer to the current weights.
  4. Save keeps the editable formula and checks in a local JSON file. Open restores that file. Copy weighing list gives you a plain-text kitchen note.

Whole grams are allocated by rounding down, then giving remaining grams to the largest fractional amounts. Ties follow ingredient order. Portion weights also add up to the batch total. Ratios stay unchanged in the saved formula.

The included formula is an editable example. This tool supplies weights, not baking instructions. On iPhone, the offered Share control can save to Files when available.
