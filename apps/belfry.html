How to ring a change

A row is one strike from each of the six bells. Bell 1 is the highest. The opening order, 1 through 6, is called rounds.

  1. Press Play score to hear the pattern. Choose a bell under Trace to follow its path through the score.
  2. Select a row in the diagram or use the row arrows. Each swap exchanges two neighboring positions from the previous row. Tapping a pair again clears it.
  3. Build your own pattern with Add a row. Return to rounds adds changes that bring every bell home.

Save keeps the editable score as a local JSON file. Export WAV records the performance as audio.
