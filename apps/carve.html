Carve

Resize a picture by removing the paths of least importance, so the subjects stay intact while the boring parts give way. Drag the right or bottom edge of the picture to carve or grow it. Paint Remove over an object and press Delete to make it vanish.

Everything runs in this one file: no uploads, no network. Drop, paste or open any image.
