A little color goes a long way.

Start with a built-in color study or open a photo. Chroma Desk finds representative colors, then gives you the last word.

  • Select a color chip, then click the image to replace it. Sampling uses the displayed image, resized if necessary.
  • For keyboard sampling, focus the image. Arrow keys move one pixel; Shift + arrow moves ten. Enter or Space applies the color.
  • Type a three- or six-digit hex color to edit a chip. Undo reverses palette changes.
  • Try two colors as text and background, then take the palette as CSS.

Images are processed in this tab and never sent anywhere. Transparent areas are placed on white. Images and colors are not saved after closing this page.
