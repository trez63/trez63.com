Crank — a planar linkage laboratory

Pin-jointed bars, grounded pivots and a turning crank. Every frame the positions are solved geometrically (each free joint is the intersection of two circles), so you can build any four-bar, straight-line or walking linkage and see exactly where it goes — and where it binds.

Tools (bottom bar)

Movedrag a joint to redesign (bar lengths follow it); drag empty space to pan; tap a bar or joint to inspect it. Pinch or wheel to zoom.
Jointtap empty space to add a joint; tap a joint to pin it to the ground (and back).
Linktap two joints to connect them with a rigid bar.
Cranktap a bar with one grounded end to make it the driver (tap again to release).
Tracetap a joint to draw its path over one full revolution (colour = speed).
Deletetap a joint or bar to remove it.

Red joints or path segments mean the bars cannot reach each other at that crank angle — the mechanism locks up there. Hollow joints are not yet constrained by two bars.
