Dibs — multi-touch attribution, in a browser tab

Paste the touch paths from your analytics path report, one per line, with how many journeys on that path converted and how many didn't. Dibs runs seven attribution models over the same data and shows where they disagree, so you can see which channels open deals, which close them, and which merely assist.

Last / First / Linear / Position (40-20-40) / Decay are the rule-of-thumb models. Markov builds a transition graph of your paths and credits each channel by its removal effect: how many conversions vanish when the channel is deleted from the graph. Shapley treats channels as players in a cooperative game and pays each its average marginal contribution over every coalition — the game-theory standard behind "data-driven" attribution, computed exactly (up to 12 channels).

Tap a channel chip to highlight it everywhere. Tap a model to sort the bars and table by it.
