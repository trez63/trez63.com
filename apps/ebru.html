Ebrupaper marbling

Ebru — paper marbling

Ink drops float on the surface; every new drop pushes the old ones aside, and every rake, comb, wave and swirl drags the whole picture with it. Nothing here is simulated fluid: each gesture is a closed-form deformation applied to every point of every drop, so it is exact, instant and perfectly reproducible from the saved gesture list.

DropTap to place a drop (size slider = radius). Sprinkle throws a handful.
RakeDrag a single stylus through the ink; curved paths work.
CombDrag a comb whose teeth span the paper (size = tooth spacing). Pull down then up for chevrons.
WaveA comb whose pull alternates along its length — bouquets and feathers.
SwirlPress where the vortex should sit, drag left or right to spin it.
