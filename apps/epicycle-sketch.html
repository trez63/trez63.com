Epicycle Sketch

Draw a closed shape. The app computes its discrete Fourier series and rebuilds it with rotating circles (epicycles). The same coefficients become a harmonic sound.

  • Mouse / Touch – draw path (release to finish)
  • Space – play / pause animation
  • C – clear & enter draw mode
  • R – random parametric shape
  • P – toggle sound
  • ↑ / ↓ – more / fewer harmonics
  • ← / → – slower / faster
  • T – theme
  • S – save JSON
  • O – open JSON
  • H or ? – this help

Tip: keep drawings reasonably smooth. More harmonics = finer detail, heavier computation.
