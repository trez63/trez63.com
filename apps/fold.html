Fold

Pick any of 18 Platonic, Archimedean and prism solids and watch it unfold into a flat net. Shuffle grows a different spanning tree of faces (a cube alone has 11 distinct nets). Tap a face on the model to make it the root of the net. Set the edge length and Print a tabbed net to cut out and glue.

Solid lines are cuts; dashed lines are folds. Stats show vertices, edges, faces, dihedral angles, paper area and the volume your model will enclose.

Keyboard (not while typing in a field):
Space play/pause · F fold · U unfold · N shuffle net · [ ] previous/next solid · ←→↑↓ turn · T tabs · P print · D theme · Ctrl/⌘ S save · Ctrl/⌘ O open · Ctrl/⌘ / help · Esc close

Save/Open keep the solid, this exact net, fold, size and view as a JSON file. Downloads work when the file is opened directly (the artifact viewer blocks page downloads).
