Print & cut.

Use ordinary paper, ideally 80–120 gsm. Print at actual size, measure the ruler, then cut the solid outline.

Fold & glue.

Place printed side down. Fold sides in; glue the shaded patches now facing up. Fold the bottom up and press.

Fill & give.

Let the glue dry, tuck your note inside, then fold the top down. A small piece of tape makes a lovely seal.

A few things before your first fold

This is a handmade envelope template, not a postal sizing guide. The dimensions describe the outside front panel; seams and paper thickness reduce the usable space. Try a scrap sheet before using special paper.

Print on one side. Fold lines and glue marks end up on the back. Some printers force scaling: select actual size, disable headers and footers, and confirm the ruler measures 50 mm. Screen previews are not life-size.
