Give your story a fork.

  1. Start with the sample, or replace its words with yours. Choose a scene from the rail to edit it.
  2. Add a choice, write its label, and choose where it leads. A scene with no choices is an ending.
  3. Use Read & play to try the routes. Back retraces one step; Start again returns to your opening scene.

The scene check points out missing destinations and scenes that cannot be reached from the beginning. Loops are allowed. A reading route can hold 100 visits; then go back or start again.

Removing a scene clears choices that led to it. Undo restores your last 30 edits. Changes to scenes or links restart the reading route when needed.

Save downloads every scene, choice and current reading position in one file. Open restores it. On iPhone, Share may offer Save to Files. Undo history is temporary and is not included.

Use Tab and Enter or Space for buttons, and the normal editing keys in text fields. There are no custom keyboard shortcuts.
