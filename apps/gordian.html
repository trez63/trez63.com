The Alexander polynomial is computed from the diagram with Fox calculus and a fraction‑free (Bareiss) determinant over ℤ[t]. It is an invariant: flip a crossing and you may change the knot; smooth or stretch the drawing and Δ stays put.

Gordian

A knot‑theory sketchpad. Draw one closed loop with a finger or mouse; wherever it crosses itself the strands alternate over/under. Tap any crossing to flip it. Gordian then computes invariants that do not depend on how the knot is drawn, and looks the result up in a table of prime knots up to 7 crossings plus a few torus and composite knots.

Alternate re‑assigns every crossing so the strand alternates over, under, over… (the most tangled reading of your drawing). Descend makes the first pass at every crossing the over‑pass, which always unties to the unknot, however messy. Mirror flips every crossing (the mirror image, same Δ). Random knot draws a random smooth loop and reads it alternately.
