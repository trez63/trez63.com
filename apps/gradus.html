    Gradus

    A cantus firmus (lower staff by default) and a counterpoint in whole notes, after Fux's Gradus ad Parnassum (1725). Every measure shows the interval between the voices; the list below the music names each broken rule. Compose writes a fresh counterpoint that satisfies the rules; drag any note with a finger or mouse to write your own and watch the checks update.

    Rules checked: consonances only (no 2nds, 4ths, 7ths, tritones); begin on a perfect consonance, end on a unison or octave approached by step; no parallel fifths or octaves; no voice crossing; no leaps of a 7th, tritone or beyond an octave. Warnings: hidden fifths/octaves, unrecovered leaps, repeated notes, more than one high point, too many perfect consonances.
