ImpactForge Help

A Project holds your tasks and axis labels. Save/Open works on the whole project.

Place tasks by drag (or arrows when selected). High Impact + Low Effort = Do Now.

Full CRUD: + Task / double-click / sidebar to edit name, description, and exact Impact/Effort. Delete from the edit modal or Del key.

Axis labels and the four quadrant names are editable — click them. Defaults are Impact / Effort and Do Now / Schedule / Delegate / Eliminate.

When creating a project you choose the axis origin: Centered (midpoint cross) or Positive only with (0,0) at the bottom-left.
