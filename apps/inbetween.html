Make the in-between matter.

  1. Choose a starting curve, then drag either handle or edit its X and Y coordinates. The horizontal direction is time; the vertical direction is position.
  2. Play compares your curve with constant speed. Replay starts a fresh comparison. Move the playhead to inspect a particular moment.
  3. Y values outside 0–1 can move the specimen backward or past its destination before it settles.
  4. Copy CSS copies the duration and timing declarations. Add a transitioning property, such as transform, in your own stylesheet.

Save keeps the complete editable study. Open restores your curve and playhead, paused. Motion starts when you choose Play or Replay.

On iPhone, use the offered share control and choose Save to Files when available. A download and complete file text are also provided.

Every action is available through the visible controls. Use Tab and the usual button and input keys to work from the keyboard.
