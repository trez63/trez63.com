Iris

About 1 in 12 men and 1 in 200 women see colour differently. Iris simulates protanopia, deuteranopia, tritanopia and achromatopsia (Machado, Oliveira & Fernandes 2009, applied in linear light) and measures every pair of your palette in OKLab under each one. Pairs closer than the collision threshold are the ones a reader cannot tell apart.

Fix palette searches for the smallest change to your colours that pushes every pair past the threshold under every vision type; locked colours stay exactly as they are. Each vision type can be Enforced or Ignored; achromatopsia is ignored by default because eight distinct lightness levels rarely fit one palette. Tap a swatch to edit or lock it. The picture panel shows any image or your live camera through the chosen vision — drag the divider to compare.
