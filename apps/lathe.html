Lathe pottery wheel

Lathe

A pottery wheel in one file. The pot is a solid of revolution rendered in software on a 2D canvas — no WebGL, no libraries. Touch the clay: drag on the pot to pull the wall out or push it in; where your finger is becomes the wall. Smooth evens out lumps. Finished pots export as a watertight OBJ with real wall thickness, ready for a 3D printer.
