From page to placement.

  1. Set your destination. Use a complete http or https address and a shared campaign name. The opening example is fictional.
  2. Edit your placements. Source is the origin, such as newsletter; medium is the channel, such as email. Optional content and term tags give you more detail.
  3. Inspect the result. Each URL ribbon separates the original destination, campaign tags, and fragment. Duplicate active links are flagged so you can distinguish placements intentionally.
  4. Copy or export. Only active, valid links go into the launch sheet. Save preserves the entire workspace, including drafts and inactive placements; Open restores it.

This creates tracking tags, not analytics. Your destination's analytics setup determines what gets recorded. Tags are visible in the URL, so use campaign labels rather than private customer details.

All controls work with touch or a keyboard. There are no custom keyboard shortcuts. Native Tab, Enter, Space, and dialog Escape keep their usual behavior. No browser storage is required; save before closing the tab.
