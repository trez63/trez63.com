    Your first delivery

    1. Choose a route. Find the launch site, orange ring and striped green receiving pad in the landscape.
    2. Adjust angle and speed, then press Launch parcel. Watch where it lands. Turn on Last flight trail to compare your next shot.
    3. Land on the pad to collect its stamp. For a gold postmark, fly through the orange ring and land on the pad in the same flight.
