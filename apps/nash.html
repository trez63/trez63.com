Nash

Hex, invented by Piet Hein and independently by John Nash. You are blue: connect the left and right edges with an unbroken chain. Nash is red and wants top to bottom. Hexes can never be blocked both ways, so one of you always wins — no draws.

Nash plays by Monte-Carlo tree search with RAVE: tens of thousands of random games per move, all in this page. While it thinks, the board glows: brighter red means more of its attention, and the outlined hex is its current favourite. Hint runs the same search for you.
