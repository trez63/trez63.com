A little context, to go.

Follow through, without the noise.

  1. Choose a person to see the next action and the context behind it. Due includes today and overdue dates. Later means after today.
  2. Use Log a touch after an interaction. Add a note and set the next action/date. Your history stays with the person.
  3. Archive removes a person from active follow-ups without deleting anything. The Archived filter lets you restore them.
  4. Use Save to keep a portable JSON file. Open restores contacts and settings. There is no automatic browser storage.

On a phone, the contact card sits below the person list. Selecting a person brings the card into view. Every action has a touch control. There are no custom keyboard shortcuts.

Dates follow your device’s local calendar. This tool does not send messages, email, or reminders. It supports 100 contacts, 100 touches per person, and 1,000 touches across a workspace. Context is limited to 2,000 characters per person; a touch note to 500.

The starter people and companies are fictional examples. Start empty to create your own desk. Save before closing the tab.
