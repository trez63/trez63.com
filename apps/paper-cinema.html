How Paper Cinema works

  1. Choose a starting film and press Play. Switch between the four pictures using the thumbnails, arrow buttons or frame slider.
  2. Select Ink or Erase. Touch or drag on the grid, or use its direction buttons and apply the tool to one cell.
  3. The interlaced plate holds narrow vertical strips from all four drawings. The shutter has one clear slit for every four strips. Each 1 mm step to the right exposes the next drawing.
  4. Export print kit gives you the plate and shutter. Print at actual size. Use paper for the plate and printer-compatible clear film for the shutter, or cut out the clear slots. Align the left registration marks and slide the shutter horizontally.
  5. Save downloads the editable film and its settings. Open restores a saved JSON film.
