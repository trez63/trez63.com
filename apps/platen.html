Platen

Photograph a page, drag the four corner handles onto its corners, and Platen straightens it with a true perspective (homography) warp, then cleans it like a scanner: Color flattens shadows, Gray is a plain grayscale, Ink is adaptive black-and-white for text. Export a PNG or print. Everything runs locally; nothing leaves your device.

Tap Photo to load (on a phone this offers the camera). Auto-detect finds a bright page on a darker surface. A loupe magnifies under your finger while dragging.
