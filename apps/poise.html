Balancing a mobile

The crown beam carries two lower beams. Each lower beam carries a pair of shapes.

  1. Choose a lower beam to change its two shape weights. Both weights also contribute to the load on the crown.
  2. The turning effect of each load equals its weight times its distance from the notch. Equal turning effects balance the beam.
  3. Move the suspension notch toward the side that pulls down to shorten its arm. Nearest notch chooses the available notch closest to exact balance.

The level plan uses massless beams, with a 40 cm crown and 20 cm lower beams. Tilt preview shows which side pulls down. Its angle is illustrative.
