Look. Turn. Recognize.

  1. Keep the original shape in mind. Rotate it by the angle shown, clockwise around the board’s center.
  2. Choose the matching tile. Every option is a different rotation of that same shape.
  3. After your first answer, inspect the quarter-turn sequence. Then move to the next challenge.

A 90° turn moves the top edge to the right; 180° turns the board upside down. The larger boards offer more cells to keep track of.

Save downloads your board size, challenge, answer and score. Open restores it. Use Tab and Enter or Space for buttons; letter keys are not shortcuts.

New game or changing board size starts a fresh score. On iPhone, the offered Share control can save to Files when available.
