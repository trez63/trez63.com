Redline

Paste two versions of a contract. Redline aligns the paragraphs (even when they moved), marks every word that changed, and reads each change for what it means: amounts and durations that shifted, obligations that went from shall to may, carve-outs that were added, clauses that vanished. Material changes are red, notable ones amber. Nothing leaves the page.
