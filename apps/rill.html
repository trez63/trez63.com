Rill

A hydrology sandbox. Every brush stroke re-runs a watershed model: depressions are flood-filled, water flows downhill (D8), accumulation becomes rivers, and the basins that feed each river mouth are mapped. Use Trace and tap anywhere to see where a raindrop goes and everything that drains to that spot.
