Warp Weft

Read the cloth

Each repeat contains eight vertical warp threads (ends) and eight horizontal passes of weft (picks).

  1. Tap a crossing in the cloth, or choose its end and pick in the loom draft.
  2. Assign the warp end to one of four shafts. For each pick, press the shaft buttons to choose which ones rise. A raised shaft brings its warp yarn to the face of the cloth.
  3. Inspect the repeating sample. A face float is a run where one yarn stays on top. An unbroken run across a whole repeat continues into the next one.

Save stores the whole draft in a local JSON file. Open restores it. Export SVG keeps a scalable picture of the cloth.
