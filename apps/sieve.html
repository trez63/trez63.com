Sieve log template miner

Logs

    Sieve — turn 10,000 log lines into 20 templates

    Paste raw logs. Sieve learns the message templates (the fixed words) and the slots (<*> — ids, hosts, durations) with a streaming parse-tree clusterer (Drain), so you see what the system is actually saying instead of scrolling. Timestamps and levels are detected in any common format.

    Drag across the timeline to pick a window: every template gets a ×lift (how much more often it happens in the window than normal) and NEW if it first appeared there. That is the fastest way to answer “what changed at 08:12?”. Tap a bucket to clear the window. Tap any template for its slot value distribution, sample lines and a ready-made regex.

    Similarity controls how aggressively lines are merged: lower it to collapse noisy templates, raise it to keep near-duplicates apart.
