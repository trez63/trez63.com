A quiet place to learn to look · Works offline

Draw toward the horizon.

  1. The violet line is eye level. Edges receding left and right converge on the two marked vanishing points.
  2. Choose a form by its numbered button or tap a visible face. Move its sliders to change the nearest edge, height and projected depths.
  3. Raise the horizon to see more of the tops. When a form’s top is at or above eye level, its roof disappears from view.
  4. Export drawing saves the plate as an SVG. Switch guides off for a clean study, or leave them on for a tracing reference.

Save keeps the editable scene; Open restores it. On iPhone, the offered Share button may let you save to Files. Download and complete file text are also available.

Sliders use positions and proportions of this drawing plate, not real-world dimensions. All controls work with touch or standard keyboard navigation. Nothing moves automatically.
