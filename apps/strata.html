Daily Build 2026-11-08 — Strata

Strata org chart analytics & reorg sandbox

Import people

Paste a CSV with columns like name, title, manager, team (header names are detected; any order). Managers are referenced by name. Nothing leaves this page.

Strata

Paste any employee list and Strata draws the org chart, measures it (layers, span of control, lonely managers, deep chains) and flags bad data (unknown managers, reporting cycles). Then drag a person onto a new manager to try a reorg; every metric updates and the Changes tab keeps the diff.

Touch: tap a card to select it, tap it again to collapse or expand its team, press-and-hold then drag to move someone, pinch to zoom, drag the background to pan.
