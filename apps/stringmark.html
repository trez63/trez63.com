A shape to come back to.

  1. Select a card, or add a new shape. Give it a name you’ll recognize.
  2. Each row is a guitar string, from thick low E (6) to thin high E (1). Choose × to mute, ○ to leave open, or a numbered fret.
  3. The diagram shows a five-fret window. Change First fret to move that window and its fretted marks together. The thick top line is the nut, visible at fret 1.
  4. Mirror reverses the drawing for a left-handed view. It keeps the same string assignments.

Use notes to record finger numbers or a barre. This notebook records your shapes; it does not identify chords or play audio.

Save keeps every card, note and setting in one JSON file. Open restores it. Export sheet creates a printable SVG image of the collection. On iPhone, Share can offer Save to Files when supported.

Use Tab to move between controls, arrow keys within a string’s choices, and Space to select. There are no custom keyboard shortcuts. Undo edit returns through your last 30 edits; this temporary history is not saved.
