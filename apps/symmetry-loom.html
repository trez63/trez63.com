Symmetry Loom

Draw freehand; every stroke is instantly mirrored and rotated into perfect multi-fold symmetry around the center.

  • H or ? — toggle this help
  • T — toggle light/dark theme
  • C — clear canvas
  • U — undo last stroke
  • Space — toggle pulse animation
  • 1–5 — set folds (2/3/6/8/12)
  • M — toggle mirror

Save downloads a .json of all strokes. Open restores any previous design. Works fully offline.
