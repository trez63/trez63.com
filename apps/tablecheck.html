OFFLINE · EXACT TO THE CENT

A fair share starts here.

  1. Rename the diners. Enter each item’s full receipt-line price, then select everyone who shared it. Those diners share that item equally.
  2. Enter the tax shown on the receipt. Choose a tip percentage from 0–50%; the tip is calculated on the item subtotal before tax.
  3. Tax and tip are divided in proportion to each diner’s item share. Open a personal check to see its items, then copy the complete breakdown.

All calculations use whole cents. Extra item cents rotate through the selected diners in table order. Tax and tip use largest remainders, with table order breaking ties (tip begins at the second diner). Personal checks always add up to the bill.

Every item needs a diner. If there are no priced items, tax must be zero before personal checks can be finalized. Empty bills stay editable.

Save downloads the complete bill as JSON. Open restores it. On iPhone, use Share and Save to Files when offered. Undo restores recent edits. Changing currency labels the existing figures; no exchange conversion happens.
