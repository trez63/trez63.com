    Event log

    Paste a CSV export with case, activity and timestamp columns (comma, tab or semicolon). Any ERP, ticketing or CRM history export works. Nothing leaves this page.

    Trace

    Trace discovers the real process hidden in an event log. Every case (order, ticket, invoice) is sorted by time, consecutive steps become arrows, and the map shows how often each path was taken and how long each hand-off waited. Red arrows are the bottlenecks. The Paths slider hides rare routes; the Activities slider hides rare steps. Tap any step, arrow or variant for detail.
