    Tranche

    A liquidation-preference waterfall in one file. Enter each share class, then drag the exit value: Tranche pays senior preferences first, lets participating series share in the rest (up to their cap), and lets every series decide whether converting to common would pay more. The chart shows every class's take across all exit values, so the kinks — where a preference is covered, a cap bites, or a series converts — are visible at a glance.

    Terms

    • Multiple — preference = invested × multiple, paid before junior classes.
    • Seniority — rank 1 is paid first; equal ranks share pro-rata (pari passu).
    • Participating — after its preference, the series also shares in the remainder as-converted; capped participation stops once total payout reaches cap × invested.
    • Conversion — a non-participating or capped series converts to common when that pays more.
