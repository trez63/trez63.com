Umbra

A sun and shadow planner for any site on Earth. Place buildings and trees on a 50 m plan, pick a date and location, and watch the shadows move through the day. The colour map shows how many hours of direct sun each patch of ground receives that day — where to plant the vegetables, where the patio will be in shade, how a two-storey neighbour shades your garden in December.

Positions come from the NOAA solar equations (declination, equation of time, hour angle). Times are local standard time for the longitude's zone (no daylight saving). Shadows are cast by flat-roofed prisms and cylinders; the map samples 51 moments between sunrise and sunset.
